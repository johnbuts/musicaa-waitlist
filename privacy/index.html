Privacy Policy

Last updated May 11, 2026

Musicaa is a music discovery app currently in pre-launch. This page explains what we collect when you join the waitlist, why we collect it, and how we handle it. Plain language, no boilerplate.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you join the waitlist on the home page.
  • Optional survey responses, if you fill in the post-signup survey (which music service you use, and a 1–10 rating about how easy it is to find new music). The survey is optional — skipping it doesn't affect your spot on the waitlist.
  • A timestamp of when you signed up, so we know the order to send early-access invites.

We don't ask for your name, location, payment info, or anything else.

Why we collect it

One reason: to send you updates about Musicaa. That means progress notes while we build the app and a notification the moment early access opens. The optional survey responses help us decide which features to prioritize.

We won't email you about anything else, and we won't sell, rent, or share your email with anyone outside Musicaa.

Where it's stored

Waitlist signups are stored in a private Google Sheet that only the Musicaa team can access. Confirmation emails are sent through Gmail.

Advertising measurement (Meta)

We run ads on Facebook and Instagram to find people who might want Musicaa. To know which ads are working, we tell Meta when someone signs up — that's the only event we share. We do this two ways:

  • Meta Pixel — a small script that runs in your browser when you visit the home page. It sets a cookie and pings Meta if you submit the waitlist form.
  • Meta Conversions API — a server-to-server notification we send from our backend when a signup succeeds.

What Meta receives: your email address (SHA-256 hashed before it leaves us — Meta can never read it in plain text), the page URL, your user agent, and the Meta cookies that Meta itself set on you. We never share your survey responses, your unhashed email, or anything else.

How to opt out: use your browser's tracking-prevention setting (Safari and Firefox enable this by default), block connect.facebook.net in any ad blocker, or visit your Meta ad preferences page. Server-side events (Conversions API) can be opted out of by deleting your record entirely — email teams@musicaa.io.

How long we keep it

We keep your email until either (a) Musicaa launches and you've decided whether to use it, or (b) you ask us to delete it — whichever comes first. If we ever shut the project down, we'll delete the entire list.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you the data we have on you (it's just your email, signup timestamp, and any optional survey answers).
  • Delete it permanently. We'll do this within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Stop emailing you without deleting your data — useful if you want to stay on the list but pause updates.

Email teams@musicaa.io for any of the above. Include the email address you signed up with so we can find your record.

Children

Musicaa isn't directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has signed up, email us and we'll remove the record.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we'll update this page and bump the “Last updated” date at the top. For material changes — like adding a new way we use your email — we'll also email everyone on the list before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: teams@musicaa.io.
